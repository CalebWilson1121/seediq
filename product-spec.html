Commercial Thesis

SeedIQ converts dealer agronomy, field-specific environmental data and seed-product characteristics into both a repeatable placement process and a sales-enablement engine for seed-company reps. The scoring engine remains deterministic and auditable; AI explains recommendations, compares alternatives and supports the dealer's final judgment. Dealer overrides become proprietary feedback data that can improve future placement models.
